Features

Everything junked, nothing missed

SweptPC targets every common source of wasted disk space so you don't have to hunt for it yourself.

Windows Temp Files

Clears %TEMP%, %TMP% and C:\Windows\Temp in seconds. Recovers gigabytes that Windows quietly hoards over time.

Browser Cache

Chrome, Edge and Firefox cache cleaned in one scan. Frees up hundreds of megabytes without touching your passwords or bookmarks.

System Logs

Old CBS and setup logs older than 30 days removed automatically. These files accumulate silently after Windows updates.

Recycle Bin

Empties all drives with a single click. SweptPC scans and shows you exactly how much space is in the bin before cleaning.

App Cache

Teams and Discord cache cleared to reclaim lost gigabytes. Both apps are known to balloon their cache folders over weeks of use.

Scan First

Always preview how much space each category holds before cleaning. Nothing is deleted without your confirmation — ever.
